1. Group Contract (Group: 10%)
   Assessment 1: Group Contract
   Due Date: refer to Learnline
   Weighting: 10%
   Group Size: 4 students
   Overview
   Before proceeding with the remaining group assessments in this unit, your team must establish a
   formal Group Contract and choose one of the approved project themes (see the Approved Project
   Themes and Requirements on Learnline).
   This first group assessment is a low-stakes assessment designed to ensure your team has a shared
   understanding of how you will work together across Assessment 2 and Assessment 4. The group
   contract must be written collaboratively as a Markdown document (.md) in a shared GitHub
   repository, giving every team member early practice with the Git-based living document workflow and
   Markdown document authoring that underpin the Architectural Decision Records required in later
   assessments.
   This is not a template-filling exercise. You are expected to have genuine discussions as a team and
   produce a document that reflects your agreed-upon terms for collaborative, professional, and ethical
   group work. The commit history of your contract will be assessed as evidence of shared authorship
   and iterative refinement.
   Relationship to Assessment 2 and Assessment 4
   This assessment supersedes both Assessment 2 (Group Project Report 1) and Assessment 4 (Group Project
   Report 2) in the sense that it must be completed first and establishes the foundation for all
   subsequent group work. The GitHub repository created for this contract should be the same
   repository used for the subsequent assessments. The skills practised here (collaborative Markdown
   authoring, commit-based contribution tracking, and maintaining a living document) directly prepare
   you for the Architectural Decision Records required in those assessments.
   Learning outcomes
   This assessment primarily addresses:
   LO5. Demonstrate problem-solving techniques through creative thinking strategies.
   LO6. Document the development process using industry standard documentation processes.
   Deliverable
   A single Markdown file (e.g., GROUP-CONTRACT-Group-Number.md) in the root of your shared GitHub
   repository. In addition to clearly stating your chosen project theme, the contract must include at least
   two sections:
   Section 1. Terms and conditions of group work
   Your team must discuss, agree upon, and document the terms and conditions under which you will
   work together. These should be specific, actionable, and enforceable, not generic platitudes.
   Topics to address include, but are not limited to:
   • Group allocation: How should grades be awarded to each member? Will all members
   receive the same mark, or will you differentiate based on contribution? What
   constitutes a fair contribution?
   • Communication: What tools will you use (e.g., Teams, GitHub Issues)? What are the
   expected response times? How will you run meetings or stand-ups?
   • Task ownership: How will tasks be assigned and tracked? What happens if someone
   falls behind or is unavailable?
   • Diverse working styles: How will you accommodate different schedules, time zones,
   learning paces, or personal circumstances?
   • Conflict resolution: What is your escalation pathway if a disagreement cannot be
   resolved within the group? At what point do you involve the teaching team?
   • Academic integrity: How will you ensure submitted work does not breach the
   University’s academic integrity policies? How will AI-generated code be reviewed,
   attributed, and understood by all members?
   Section 2. Provisional milestones and checkpoints
   Using the released specifications for Assessment 2 and Assessment 4, your team must produce a
   provisional project plan that includes:
   • Key milestones with target dates for each assessment
   • Task breakdowns showing how the assessed topics will be tackled and by whom
   • Internal checkpoints or review points where the team will assess progress
   • Integration points where individual work will be merged and tested together
   This plan is provisional and is expected to evolve throughout the semester. Updating it in response to
   reality is encouraged and demonstrates mature project management. What matters is that your team
   has thought critically about the work ahead and has a starting plan to work from.
   A note on this assessment
   This is intentionally a low-stakes assessment. Its purpose is to give your team the best possible start
   by making implicit expectations explicit before the technical work begins. Teams that invest genuine
   effort here consistently perform better in subsequent assessments; not because of the marks, but
   because they have already navigated the hard conversations about how to work together. Treat this
   as a real professional exercise: the best contracts are the ones you hope you never need to enforce,
   but are glad you have when things get difficult.
   Submission
   Submit your team's GitHub repository URL via Learnline no later than the stated due date of this
   assignment.
   • The contract must be a Markdown (.md) file in your shared GitHub repository
   • All team members must have at least one commit, making a substantial and meaningful
   contribution to the contract
   • The repository must be accessible to the teaching team (add as collaborators or make
   public)
   Further:
   • Email submissions will not be accepted.
   • Do not submit multiple documents.
   • Late submission penalty may apply according CDU policies.
   You will not only be evaluated for the output you produce, but also for the authentic and
   verifiable process you went through in completing this assessment. Your team must actively
   collaborate to demonstrate your thought process on Microsoft Teams. Interactions on other
   platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, Discord, etc.) will not be admitted as evidence of your thought
   process.
