Section 1: Terms and conditions of group work

Group allocation: After submitting the assessments, all members would initially get the same marks based on the overall submission quality. However, individual marks may be adjusted if there are significant differences in the contribution of the team members. A fair contribution from each member would indicate that they attended the group meetings and adhered to the provided schedule. In case they can’t follow the schedule strictly or run late into meetings, they must notify the team beforehand on the Teams channel.
